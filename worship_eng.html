THE STORY BEHIND SHOUT

The Story of SHOUT

SHOUT is kept as a distinct English worship archive within The Creative Archive.

01 · A VOICE OF PRAISE

Let praise be heard

SHOUT carries an outward, celebratory expression of worship — songs written to lift the heart, gather voices and proclaim faith.

02 · FAITH IN MOTION

From confession to declaration

The songs move between quiet gratitude and bold declaration, holding together personal testimony and communal praise.

03 · BEYOND ONE LANGUAGE

Worship without borders

English gives this collection another doorway into the same foundation: worship, grace, hope and the name of Jesus.

04 · THE ARCHIVE

A separate English worship chapter

SHOUT remains separate from Korean Worship so each worship collection can keep its own album artwork, story and complete lyric archive.
