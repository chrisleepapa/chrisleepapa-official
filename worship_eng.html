SHOUT

Praise beyond language. Worship without borders.
An English worship collection of praise, faith, gratitude and testimony.

THE PROJECT

SHOUT is an English-language worship project born from a desire to let praise become a voice that can reach beyond one language and one place.

ENGLISH WORSHIP15 TRACKSCHRIS LEE.PAPA

THE JOURNEY

A Voice of Praise

SHOUT carries a more outward, celebratory expression of worship—songs written to lift the heart, gather voices and proclaim faith.

Faith in Motion

From personal testimony to communal praise, the songs move between quiet gratitude and bold declaration.

Beyond One Language

English gives this collection a different doorway into the same foundation: worship, grace, hope and the name of Jesus.

Worship Without Borders

SHOUT is preserved as a distinct English worship archive within The Creative Archive.
